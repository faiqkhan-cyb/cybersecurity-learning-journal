##  Key Learnings & Core Concepts

- **Defensive Security (Blue Team):** Protecting an organization's infrastructure by monitoring, detecting, and mitigating cyber threats.
- **SOC Operations:** Understanding the role of Security Operations Centers in analyzing logs and responding to incidents.
- **Incident Response Cycle:** Detecting malicious activity, isolating threats, and restoring systems.
- **Digital Forensics & Detection:** Analyzing evidence from security events to understand attack vectors.
